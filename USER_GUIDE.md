# Food Stand POS - User Guide

## 🎯 For Cashiers and Staff

This guide explains how to use the POS system for daily operations.

## 📱 Opening the POS App

### First Time Setup
1. Open Chrome on your Android device
2. Go to: `http://[server-address]:8080/pos.html`
3. Tap the menu (⋮) → "Add to Home screen"
4. Name it "Food Stand POS"
5. Tap "Add"

### Daily Use
1. Tap the "Food Stand POS" icon on your home screen
2. App opens in full-screen mode
3. Ready to take orders!

## 🍔 Taking an Order

### Step 1: Select Items
- Tap menu items to add them to the cart
- Items are organized by category (Main, Sides, Drinks)
- Each tap adds one item

### Step 2: Adjust Quantities
- Use **+** button to increase quantity
- Use **−** button to decrease quantity
- Item automatically removes when quantity reaches 0

### Step 3: Review Order
- Check the cart on the right side (or bottom on phone)
- Verify all items and quantities are correct
- See the total amount at the bottom

### Step 4: Process Payment
1. Tap the green **"Pay Cash"** button
2. Enter the cash amount received from customer
3. Or use quick amount buttons ($5, $10, $20, $50, $100)
4. Or tap **"Exact"** if customer has exact change
5. Change amount displays automatically
6. Tap **"Complete"** to finish the sale

### Step 5: Give Change
- Tell customer the change amount shown
- Give change to customer
- Transaction is complete!

## 💡 Tips for Fast Service

### Quick Ordering
- Memorize common item locations
- Use both hands on tablet
- Double-tap for quantity 2
- Keep tapping for more

### Common Orders
- Combo meals: Add main + side + drink quickly
- Multiple same items: Tap once, then use + button

### Payment Shortcuts
- **Exact button**: Customer has exact amount
- **Quick amounts**: Common bills ($5, $10, $20)
- **Round numbers**: Easy to calculate

## 🔄 Understanding Online/Offline Status

### Online (Green Dot)
- Connected to internet
- Sales save immediately to server
- Menu updates automatically
- Everything syncs in real-time

### Offline (Red Dot)
- No internet connection
- **POS still works normally!**
- Sales save to device
- Will sync automatically when back online

### What to Do When Offline
**Nothing different!** Just keep taking orders normally.
- All sales are saved safely on the device
- When internet returns, sales upload automatically
- You don't need to do anything special

## ❌ Fixing Mistakes

### Wrong Item Added
- Tap the **−** button until quantity is 0
- Item disappears from cart

### Wrong Quantity
- Use **+** or **−** buttons to adjust
- Or remove and re-add the item

### Need to Start Over
- Tap the red **"Clear"** button
- Confirms before clearing
- Cart empties completely

### Wrong Payment Amount
- Tap **"Cancel"** in payment screen
- Re-enter correct amount
- Or tap **"Complete"** again

## 🚨 Common Issues

### Menu Not Loading
**Solution:**
1. Check if you're connected to WiFi
2. Close and reopen the app
3. If still not working, contact manager

### Can't Complete Payment
**Check:**
- Cash received is greater than total
- You entered a number (not blank)
- Try tapping "Complete" again

### App Seems Frozen
**Solution:**
1. Wait 5 seconds (might be processing)
2. If still frozen, close app completely
3. Reopen from home screen
4. Your last sale is saved

### Lost Internet Connection
**Don't worry!**
- Red dot appears at top
- Keep working normally
- Sales save to device
- Will sync when connection returns

## 📊 End of Day

### What You Need to Know
- All sales are automatically saved
- Manager can view reports in dashboard
- You don't need to "close out" the POS
- Just close the app when done

### If Asked for Daily Total
1. Tell manager to check dashboard
2. Or note the last transaction time
3. Manager can generate reports
